04 · The scale of the problem: 80%+ of heating systems run inefficiently; at most 52 TWh of annual waste; €3B+ wasted a year in Germany. Why he does this: "I am dedicated to steering my work towards carbon neutrality and efficiency: products that help buildings use less energy, and energy systems run on less waste, every year from here on."

## [uponor-loop] EcoMate · Closed loop | uponor/loop
How the closed loop works:
1. Boiler: sensors in the basement, connected to the boiler, measure and optimise heating energy consumption.
2. Coldest room: a sensor in the coldest room of the building reports back how the heating actually arrives.
3. Feedback: that reading closes the loop, so the boiler settings follow real conditions in the building.
4. Result: higher energy efficiency of the building, without changing how tenants live.

## [eon-models] IHC · Business models | eon/models
Four business models Anand designed and tested to bring IHC to market, so the choice of model came from evidence rather than assumption:
1. Hardware sale plus multi-year SaaS: direct sale of the hardware with a multi-year SaaS contract. The classic model, and the one most customers understood fastest.
2. Rental: a monthly fee over a multi-year term, with no upfront hardware cost. Offered as part of A/B testing; no customer chose it, which is evidence about how this customer group budgets.
3. Partner model with O2 Business: the telecoms provider brought customers, turning its sales channel into an acquisition channel.
4. Shared model with Danfoss Leanheat: E.ON One's hardware and field operations with Danfoss Leanheat's optimisation algorithm, in place of a one-time hardware sale.

## [eon-failsafe] IHC · Fail-safe | eon/failsafe
Built to fail safely: if the IHC box loses its connection, it switches to bypass mode. The outdoor temperature sensor signal passes straight to the heating system, so tenants keep their heating.
Alongside the Danfoss teams, Anand worked with E.ON's own engineering team on product development, and navigated EU energy regulation (GEG, EnWG) to launch the enterprise SaaS. The work earned the Best of Technology Award 2024.

Capstone, individual, September 2026. A commissioning and connectivity copilot for installers at small and mid-sized heat pump vendors entering the German market. It tackles two field problems: fault codes that installers cannot resolve on site, and controller connection drops that customers mistake for hardware failure.

## [ai-projects-book-bot] AI projects · Book Order Bot | ai-projects/book-bot
School Book Order Bot (Project 3, individual, August 2026). An n8n workflow that takes school book orders from a family bookshop's Telegram chat. It reads text and photographed handwritten orders, matches them against the publisher catalogue, logs each order and answers status questions. On request it consolidates open orders into one list per publisher, ready to send.
Catalogue extraction from a 44-page publisher price list PDF, a daily dashboard for the shop owner, and an audit against the EU AI Act (minimal risk, with a transparency duty) and GDPR for a possible rollout to other independent booksellers.
Tools: n8n, Telegram, OpenAI vision, Google Sheets.
